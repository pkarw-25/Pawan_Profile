Medical Imaging & Vision

NoiseAwareSwinUNet

A hybrid Swin Transformer and Restormer architecture engineered for denoising Whole-Body Diffusion-Weighted MRI scans.

NCVPRIPG 2026 (Accepted) arXiv Preprint (2026)
APPOINTMENT & LAB

Junior Research Fellow (JRF)

Institute of Infrastructure Technology Research and Management (IITRAM)

Advisor: Dr. Prasun Chandra Tripathi (Sep 2025 – Jan 2026)

How the architecture works:

  • MDTA Blocks: Multi-Dconv Head Transposed Attention applies self-attention across channels rather than spatial dimensions, keeping memory usage practical.
  • GDFN Networks: Gated-Dconv Feed-Forward Networks control signal flow to filter out non-central Rician noise without blurring clinical boundaries.
  • Diagnostic Detail: Consistently preserves small lesions and sharp tissue boundaries even at high b-values.
